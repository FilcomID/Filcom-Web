Service area

For the Jabodetabek area we handle on-site visits at your location. Outside Jabodetabek, installation and consultation are carried out remotely for clients across Indonesia, with visits scheduled as the project requires.

Work commonly done remotely: server configuration and maintenance, private cloud and mail server deployment, infrastructure monitoring, plus all website, SEO, and automation work. Work that requires a physical presence — cable runs, network device installation, hardware handling — is scheduled as a visit.

The Flow

What happens after you contact us

01 We reply within 1x24 working hours

The first reply includes a few clarifying questions: what's currently happening, how many users, and how urgent it is. No cost at this stage.

02 Initial consultation & assessment

For the Jabodetabek area this is usually a short on-site visit; outside the area it's done remotely. We review the current condition and note what needs handling first. This stage is free and non-binding.

03 Written quote

You receive a breakdown of the scope of work, the devices needed along with their specifications, the schedule, and the cost. No work starts before you approve this quote.

04 Implementation & handover

Work is scheduled to keep operational disruption to a minimum. Once complete, you receive configuration documentation and administrator access, with ongoing maintenance available as an option if needed.

Before you contact us

Is the initial consultation really free?

Yes. The initial consultation and assessment carry no cost and no commitment. Charges only apply once you approve the written quote we send.

What information should I prepare?

Just a general picture: what's currently a problem or what you want to build, roughly how many users or devices are involved, and how urgent it is. No need for technical terms — an office floor plan, a device list, or screenshots of the issue helps but is not required.

How long until you reply?

We reply within 1x24 working hours. Business hours are Monday-Friday, 09:00-17:00 WIB. Messages received outside business hours or on weekends are answered the next working day. For anything that has stopped your operations, mention it in your message so we can prioritise it.

Do I have to go through the form?

No. The form just helps organise the initial information so our reply is more on target. You can contact WhatsApp +62 877-8978-4423 directly or send an email to info@filcom.id.

Do you take on small or one-off jobs?

Yes. There's no obligation to take an ongoing contract. Many jobs are one-time, such as network repairs, server migrations, or website builds. Routine maintenance is available as an option, not a requirement.
