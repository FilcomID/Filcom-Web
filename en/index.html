Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Filcom.id handle?

Filcom.id covers six areas: network & IT infrastructure, server & data infrastructure, private cloud & digital workplace, virtualization & monitoring, IT support & managed service, plus website development, SEO, and AI automation.

Do you work with small businesses and SMEs?

Yes. Most of our clients are offices, SMEs, schools, and organisations that don't yet have an internal IT division.

Can you visit our location?

For the Jabodetabek area we offer on-site visits. Outside that area we work remotely, and visits can be scheduled as needed for the project.

How much does it cost?

Cost depends on the scale and condition of your existing infrastructure. We start with a free consultation and assessment, then provide a written quote before work begins.

Do we have to take all services at once?

No. Services can be taken individually, such as only network repair or only website development.

How long does the work take?

Small network fixes can be finished within days. Server or private cloud deployments generally take a few weeks. Company profile websites usually take 2–4 weeks, depending on how ready the materials are.
