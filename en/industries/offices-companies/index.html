Growing companies often add employees, applications, and data faster than their infrastructure can keep up. Filcom helps set up servers, virtualization, private cloud, and IT support so systems stay stable, monitored, and maintainable in the long run. The infrastructure side and the digital side are handled by one partner, so a company doesn't need to act as a go-between across vendors.

Free Consultation

Common Challenges for Offices & Companies

  • No centralized login system for users, access rights, and password policy
  • Company files scattered across individual employees' computers, with no access control
  • Too many physical servers with low utilization and high electricity costs
  • No way to know there's a server or network problem until users complain
  • IT issues handled ad hoc by staff outside their field, with no documentation
  • SaaS and cloud subscription costs keep rising as more users are added

Relevant Services

Server & Data Infrastructure

Centralized login, access rights, and controlled company file storage, plus scheduled migration of old servers without stopping operations.

Server Virtualization & IT Monitoring

Consolidating several physical servers into virtual machines to raise utilization and cut costs, with monitoring that alerts you before users complain.

Private Cloud & Digital Workplace

Cloud storage, file sharing, and email on your own domain, running on your company's own server — an alternative to ever-rising SaaS subscription costs.

IT Support & Managed Service

Routine IT handling with an agreed scope and SLA, complete with system documentation, without having to build your own IT division.

How We Get Started

01 Consultation & Assessment

We listen to your company's growth plans and review the condition of your existing servers, network, and systems, then flag the areas most likely to become a bottleneck as your user count grows.

02 Planning & Quotation

We put together a phased plan — server consolidation, centralized login, private cloud, monitoring — along with a written quote that can follow your company's budget and priorities.

03 Implementation

Work is scheduled outside busy hours and carried out in stages, with the old system kept available as a backup until the new one is proven to run.

04 Monitoring & Maintenance

Once running, the infrastructure is monitored and maintained regularly so issues are caught before they affect operations, and growth can keep being supported.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Filcom act as our full IT team?

Yes. Through our managed service, Filcom handles a company's IT infrastructure with a scope of work and SLA agreed upfront. If a company already has IT staff, we can support the internal team on specific areas such as servers, virtualization, or monitoring.

Can server or system migration be done without stopping operations?

Migrations are scheduled in stages outside busy hours. The old server keeps running as a backup until the new system is proven stable, so operations don't stop during the transition.

Can Filcom handle both infrastructure and the website at the same time?

Yes. Handling both the infrastructure side and the digital side with one team is exactly Filcom's positioning, so a company doesn't need to act as a go-between for a networking vendor and a website vendor.
