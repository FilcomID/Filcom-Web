IT Services for Schools & Foundations

Schools manage computer labs, networks, student data, and sometimes a school website, often without dedicated IT staff. Filcom helps set up school networks and servers and build websites, with work scheduled so it doesn't disrupt teaching and learning activities. The work can be taken in stages, starting with the part that's most urgent.

Free Consultation

Common Challenges for Schools

  • WiFi and network coverage is uneven across classrooms and computer labs
  • Network cabling is messy and undocumented
  • Student data and documents are scattered across individual teachers' computers, with no backup
  • The computer lab and internal applications need their own server, not piggybacking on a work computer
  • No one handles IT routinely — it only gets called in once something breaks
  • The school website doesn't show up when parents search on Google

Relevant Services

Network & IT Infrastructure

WiFi and network coverage that reaches classrooms and computer labs evenly, tidied up and documented so it's easy to trace when problems occur.

Server & Data Infrastructure

A dedicated server for the computer lab and internal applications, plus centralized storage with backup for student data and school documents.

Website, SEO & AI Automation

A school website built to be found by parents searching on Google, and easy to update yourselves for announcements and enrollment information.

IT Support & Managed Service

Routine IT handling without needing to hire dedicated IT staff, with a scheduled maintenance plan and on-call support when issues arise.

How We Get Started

01 Consultation & Assessment

We listen to your needs and review the current condition of your network, servers, and computers, then determine which area is most urgent to address first.

02 Planning & Quotation

We put together a phased plan by area — computer lab, classrooms, teachers' office — and a written quote that can be adjusted to your school's budget.

03 Implementation

Work is scheduled outside teaching hours or during holidays, with one area completed before moving to the next, so teaching and learning activities aren't disrupted.

04 Monitoring & Maintenance

Once running, we monitor and maintain it regularly so issues are caught before they disrupt school activities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the work disrupt teaching and learning activities?

No. Work is scheduled outside teaching hours or during holidays, and carried out in stages by area so classrooms and labs remain usable throughout the process.

Can we start with just part of it first?

Yes. Services can be taken individually or in stages — for example, fixing the computer lab network first, or just building a school website — then adding other parts later according to budget.

Does Filcom serve schools outside Jabodetabek?

For the Jabodetabek area we provide on-site visits. Outside that area we work remotely, with visits that can be scheduled according to project needs.
