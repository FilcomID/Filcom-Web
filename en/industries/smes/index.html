Many SMEs run their operations with makeshift tools and no IT staff. Filcom helps get the basics right — networking, computers, data, and websites — at a cost you can budget for. The work can be taken in stages, starting with the part that's most urgent.

Free Consultation

Common Challenges for SMEs

  • Office internet or WiFi frequently drops or slows down during peak hours
  • Business files scattered across individual phones and laptops, with no backup or access control
  • Still using free email instead of an email with your own domain
  • Not yet able to hire full-time IT staff, but still need routine handling
  • Already has a website, but it never shows up in Google search
  • A lot of time spent on manual recaps, copy-pasting data, and routine reports

Relevant Services

IT Support & Managed Service

Routine IT handling and on-call emergency support without needing to hire full-time IT staff, with a monthly cost you can budget for.

Network & IT Infrastructure

Stable, even office internet and WiFi, tidied up and documented from the start so it's easy to trace when problems occur.

Website, SEO & AI Automation

A website built to be found by prospective customers on Google, plus automation for repetitive work like recaps and reports.

Private Cloud & Digital Workplace

Shared file storage, email with your own domain, and secure access from outside the office without ever-rising subscription costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to take all the services at once?

No. Services can be taken individually or in stages — for example, starting with fixing the network first, or just building a website. You can add other parts later once your business is ready.

How much does it cost for a small business?

Cost depends on the scale and condition of your existing infrastructure. We do a free initial consultation and assessment, then provide a written quote before work begins. We don't display fixed prices because every business's needs are different.

Does Filcom serve small businesses?

Yes. Most of our clients are offices, SMEs, schools, and organizations that don't yet have an internal IT division.
