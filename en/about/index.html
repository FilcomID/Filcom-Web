One partner for infrastructure and digital

Filcom.id is an end-to-end IT solutions provider for SMEs, offices, schools, and organisations across Indonesia — from networking, servers, and private cloud, to websites, SEO, and AI automation. One partner for both the infrastructure side and the digital side.

The working principle is simple: IT and Digital Solutions for Businesses That Can't Afford Downtime. Infrastructure, cloud, websites, SEO, and automation are handled by the same team, so clients don't have to act as the go-between across vendors.

Two capability pillars

Filcom presents two capability pillars — two divisions that support each other: one holds the infrastructure foundation, the other holds the web, search, and automation side.

Pillar A — Infrastructure & Systems

Handles the foundation side: networking, servers, and data infrastructure. Focused on systems that are stable, monitored, and maintainable long-term — from office network design to private cloud deployment and server virtualization. Experienced in handling infrastructure for offices, schools, and organisations, with an emphasis on tidy documentation and a handover that the internal team can manage.

  • Office networking: LAN and WiFi design & installation, optimization & troubleshooting, administration, monitoring and maintenance
  • Servers: Windows Server & Linux, Active Directory & Domain Services, file server, application server, deployment & migration
  • Private cloud & digital workplace: Nextcloud, cloud storage, file sharing, mail server on your own domain, remote access
  • Virtualization: VMware, Proxmox, and Hyper-V, virtual machine deployment, physical server consolidation
  • IT monitoring: server and network monitoring, performance & availability, automatic notifications when there's a disruption
  • IT support: remote and on-site support, preventive maintenance, monthly managed IT service, technical consultation

Pillar B — Web, SEO & AI Automation

Handles the side the market sees: websites, search engine visibility, and process automation. 8+ years of experience in IT and digital, with a focus on WordPress, Next.js/React, technical SEO, GA4, and building AI-based automation systems.

  • WordPress & Elementor, Next.js / TypeScript / React, Shopify
  • Technical SEO + GEO/AEO — brand visibility in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini
  • GA4, Google Tag Manager, Looker Studio, Google Search Console
  • Automation: n8n, Claude API, MCP, document workflows & automated reporting
  • Supporting infrastructure: Cloudflare, Docker, self-hosted VPS
  • Security: Wazuh SIEM (deployment & monitoring)
  • Design: Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, Figma, Canva
  • Database & data: SQL and data processing for stakeholder reporting
